# Customer Discovery: Interview Guide (Phase 0.4)

Goal: 10-15 conversations of 20-30 minutes. Learn what hurts and what people already pay for. Do not pitch.
Rules: ask about past behavior, not hypotheticals; never mention prices first; take notes verbatim.

## Warm-up (2 min)
- What kind of sites do you run on Umbraco (agency, in-house, how many, which version)?
- Who handles redirects and 404s, and how often?

## Current workflow (8 min)
1. Walk me through the last time you had to deal with redirects (migration, relaunch, SEO request).
2. How do you find broken URLs today (Search Console, logs, the 404 log in our plugin, other tools)?
3. What do you do with a 404 once you find it? Who decides the target?
4. What was the most annoying or slowest part?
5. What have you tried to solve it (other packages, spreadsheets, scripts)? Why did it not stick?

## Value and money (6 min)
6. What does a bad redirect or a lost-traffic week cost you or your client?
7. Have you paid for any Umbraco package or SaaS tool? Which, how much, who approved it?
8. Who signs off on a tool purchase (you, a lead, the client)? How long does it usually take?

## Concepts to react to (6 min, show only after the above)
Ask for reactions, then "would you use it this month?" and "what would make it not worth it?"
- Suggested redirect targets for 404s (ranked, with confidence).
- 404 Intelligence: trends, noisy-bot filtering, "fix these 10 first".
- Migration assistant (import from old site / sitemap, validate chains and loops).
- Health scanner (redirect chains, loops, dead targets) with a report.
- Multi-site or multi-environment views for agencies.

## Close (2 min)
- Is there something I did not ask that I should have?
- May I follow up when an early version exists? Could I show you a free alpha?
- Do you know someone else with the same problem?

## After each call (5 min)
Fill the tracker row the same day. Tag pains: finding, deciding targets, volume, migration, reporting, other.

## Signal scoring
Strong: a specific recent story, workaround already in place, someone has paid for a similar tool, asks to try it.
Weak: "sounds nice", no recent incident, no budget owner. Count strong signals only toward Gate B.
